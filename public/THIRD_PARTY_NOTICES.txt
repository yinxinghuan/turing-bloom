The AlterU adaptation preserves the Gray-Scott reaction-diffusion shader,
parameter-space mapping, GPU ping-pong simulation, touch seeding, and view
transform concepts. It adds mobile composition, onboarding, a gesture-driven
specimen maturity loop, synthesized feedback, and offscreen resource suspension.
